Questionnaire 12

1. In Pattern mode, what do the other instruments play?
Last selected pattern.

2. Where does the end of the song, and so the loop point, sit?
Last placed block.

3. Do drum hits have a length, or are they all the same short hit?
Drum hits do not have length. They last the entire duration of the sound effect.

4. Should the editor view scroll to follow the playhead during playback?
No.

5. Should the editors be zoomable?
No.
