Questionnaire 11

1. In Pattern mode, do the other instruments play along while you edit one?
Yes, unless it's isolated.

2. Does the mixer have a master volume?
Yes.

3. Can more than one instrument be isolated at once?
No, doesn't make sense. But isolating basically means muting everything else, nothing else. It's not really a special thing. Maybe the button is sort of highlighted to indicate isolation but that's it.

4. Does Song mode loop back to the start when it reaches the end?
Yes.

5. Should the app ask to save when you close it with unsaved changes?
Yes.
