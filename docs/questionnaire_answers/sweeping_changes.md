Sweeping changes (written by the user in chat on 2026-11-09, after the first run of the app; not a numbered questionnaire)

Lot of changes needed.

ALL PAGES

1. Nothing should have scrollbars. Nothing.

2. Vocals of a midi should map to keys.

3. There should be 7 tabs: Rhythm, Lead, Bass, Drums, Keys, Song, Mixer

4. You should be able to horizontally zoom in and out of the song page and the instrument pages.

5. Things should just feel more natural, Claude. Just stuff that a DAW should have. The selections, the copying, all that.

MIXER PAGE

1. The "import midi export wav" etc buttons on the mixer page sit too snuggly to the master volume bar.

2. BPM should not be a slide bar.

3. Turning the meter to 3/4 shouldn't delete everything in the last bar. That's just wrong. It should remember it somehow.

SONG PAGE

1. The patterns should not be solid black. They should have a black outline and should be gray inside.

2. The pattern names should stick to the left side of the block.

3. Pattern placing. You shouldn't pick the instrument at the top. When you click on an empty spot it is that instrument anyway. It's redundant.

4. You should be able to horizontally zoom in and out of the song page. 

5. Left clicking on an empty spot should do nothing. You should right click and then, from a selection, choose the pattern you want to lay down.

6. When you copy a pattern, it should paste to the slot next to it.

7. You should be able to CTRL select or rectangle select multiple patterns and paste them. It should just be natural, namsayin?

FOR DRUMS, GUITARS AND KEYS

1. This is a big problem. I won't deliberate too hard on it, I just expect you to take care of it: The settings take up too much space. They should be little buttons. Everything does not need to be said, I'll know what they do. 20 places for patterns? Entire large row for length etc? Make it right. Make it more compact. The instrument should take precedent. Don't go overboard, just make them know their place.

2. When you select a note length, the guiding lines should show that note length. They shouldn't stay quarter notes fixed.
