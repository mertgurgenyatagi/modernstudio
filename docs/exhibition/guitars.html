Guitar page: twelve layouts

One page serves Bass, Rhythm Guitar and Lead Guitar, because the three differ only in their strings: four for Bass, six for the two guitars. A choice of instrument sits on the page and everything else is shared. Fonts and palette are the decided Bone and black. Only the arrangement changes. Each design shows the editor for one of the three, one bar of sixteenth steps, seven frets, and sample notes, pattern numbers and envelope values made up to fill the screen. Each design is hand written.
