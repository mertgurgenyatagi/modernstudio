Song page: ten layouts

Fonts and palette are the decided Bone and black, the same in every design. Only the arrangement changes. The song shown is sample content: twelve bars in 4/4, five lanes, the playhead at bar 6. Each design is hand written.

02Tabbed tray

The lanes stay low and plain, with only a name beside each. One tray under them, with a tab per instrument, shows the chosen instrument’s twenty patterns as large tiles. Play sits in the corner above the names.

05Focus lane
