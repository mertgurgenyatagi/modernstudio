Drums page: ten layouts

Fonts and palette are the decided Bone and black, the same in every design. Only the arrangement changes. Every design has the five rows from top to bottom as crash, open hi-hat, hi-hat, snare, kick, and the same sample beat in 4/4, shown as sixteenth steps. The beat, the pattern numbers and the envelope values are sample content. Each design is hand written.
