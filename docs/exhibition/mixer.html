Mixer, seventeen designs

Every design holds the same content: five channels with volume, mute, isolate and a level meter, the master volume, BPM and meter. Only the arrangement changes. The look is fixed to Bone and black: Piazzolla for titles, Golos Text for text, Fragment Mono for figures.

Design 02, the Ledger, was picked. Twenty were planned and the work stopped at seventeen. Designs 01 to 08 were looked at in a browser. Designs 09 to 17 were written but never viewed, so treat them as unchecked drafts.

The sliders, mute, isolate and BPM controls work. The meters move while Play is on and stop when it is off.
